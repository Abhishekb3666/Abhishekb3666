## About me

I'm an entrepreneur who's happiest when I'm three projects deep and shipping
the middle one. I co-founded **The Dune Productions**, I work as an AI/ML
engineer, and most of what you'll find here is the residue of me trying to
figure out what AI actually makes possible versus what's just hype.

I don't collect ideas, I ship them. If something looks broken, undercooked,
or unclaimed, that's usually where I'm most interested. This page is one of
those projects, it rebuilds a small piece of itself every couple of days.

```txt
while (curious) {

  ship();
  learn(what_broke);
}
```
